01 / ABOUT

Good information
changes what's possible.

My work is rooted in a simple conviction: information is most valuable when it helps people understand a situation, make informed choices and improve the way programmes are delivered.

Across humanitarian and development settings, I have supported monitoring, evaluation, accountability, research and information management functions. My experience spans the design of data systems and assessment tools, field-level data quality assurance, analysis, visualisation, reporting and the practical use of evidence.

I bring together a background in Statistics and Information Management with hands-on experience supporting teams, partners and programmes. I am especially interested in making complex data useful, accessible and responsible — while keeping people, protection and accountability at the centre.
