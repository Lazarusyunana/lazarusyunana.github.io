ABOUT / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PRACTICE

Good work begins with good questions.

My professional journey sits at the intersection of statistics, information management, humanitarian programming and evidence-informed decision-making.

MY APPROACH

Understand the need. Design the system. Make the evidence useful.

I believe information should serve people and decisions — not become an end in itself.

My work has involved supporting humanitarian and development programmes with monitoring, evaluation, accountability, research and information management. I work across the information cycle: understanding what needs to be known, designing tools and processes, supporting collection and quality assurance, analysing results, and presenting findings clearly.

My academic foundation in Statistics and postgraduate studies in Information Technology and Information Management inform a practical approach that combines analytical thinking with the realities of programme delivery.

I am especially interested in responsible data practice, protection-sensitive information management, accessible analytical products and strengthening the capacity of teams and partners to use information well.
